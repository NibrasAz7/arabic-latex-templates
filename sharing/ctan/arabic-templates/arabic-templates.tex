\begin{abstract}
\noindent The \texttt{arabic-templates} package is a collection of 91 Arabic/RTL
LaTeX templates organized into 10 categories: Academic \& Research, Business \&
Legal, Project Management, Education \& Personal, Presentations, Letters \&
Correspondence, Finance \& Accounting, Marketing \& Communications, Personal \&
Life, and Technical \& Engineering. All templates use XeLaTeX with the Amiri
font and \texttt{polyglossia}/\texttt{bidi} for full right-to-left Arabic
typesetting with mixed English support.
\end{abstract}

\section{Requirements}
\label{sec:requirements}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Compiler:} XeLaTeX (required for \texttt{fontspec} and \texttt{bidi})
    \item \textbf{Font:} Amiri (\texttt{Amiri-Regular.ttf}) --- available in TeX Live and MiKTeX
    \item \textbf{Packages:} \texttt{polyglossia}, \texttt{bidi}, \texttt{fontspec}, and standard packages (\texttt{graphicx}, \texttt{booktabs}, \texttt{tabularx}, \texttt{tcolorbox}, \texttt{tikz}, etc.)
\end{itemize}

\section{Usage}
\label{sec:usage}

Each template is self-contained in its own directory under \texttt{templates/}.
To use a template:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Navigate to the desired template directory, e.g.\ \texttt{templates/Academic\_and\_Research/thesis\_template/}
    \item Compile with XeLaTeX (run twice for TOC and references):
    \begin{verbatim}
xelatex main.tex
xelatex main.tex
    \end{verbatim}
    \item For templates with a bibliography, also run \texttt{bibtex main} between the XeLaTeX runs.
\end{enumerate}
